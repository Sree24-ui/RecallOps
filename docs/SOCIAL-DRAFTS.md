## LinkedIn

I built RecallOps for the Anakin Forge Hackathon as sole contributor Sree24-ui.

Electronics recalls are about individual units. Two identical-looking power banks can have different eligibility because of their serial numbers, original sellers, or purchase dates.

RecallOps connects official recall evidence to inventory decisions. Anakin Search discovers sources, URL Scraper extracts structured conditions, and deterministic code evaluates each unit. Affected inventory gets an internal hold, a case, staff tasks, and an evidence-backed action packet.

Live verification covered four sample units against CPSC and INIU notices: one affected, two excluded, and one needing review. A separate Amazon Wire call enriched a related listing without inferring a physical serial.

Monitoring created a real paused monitor and queued a manual run. Its separate source scrape completed; provider monitor completion and public webhook delivery remain unverified.

The local app also includes a clearly labeled, 24-unit controlled Judge demonstration. It makes evidence and uncertainty visible, without claiming a product is safe.
