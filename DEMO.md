# How the RecallOps demo works

Use the live site at https://recallops-nine.vercel.app. A public notice describes a recalled product; a physical unit needs facts from its owner. The catalogue can be demonstrated immediately. An end-to-end inventory investigation needs genuine inventory supplied by you.

## Two-to-three-minute walkthrough

| Time | Show | Explain |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:20 | Public catalogue | RecallOps connects official recall evidence to individual inventory decisions. |
| 0:20–0:55 | Search INIU BI-B41 or CPSC 26-135; expand details; open original notice | The catalogue is a dated, selected CPSC API snapshot. Current remedy instructions come from the original notice because API fields can be incomplete or mismatched. |
| 0:55–1:15 | Owner workspace; sign in off-screen | Public browsing requires no key. Inventory and internal actions are private. Keep the owner key out of the recording. |
| 1:15–1:45 | Import review; download the blank CSV | Use actual asset IDs, observed product/serial details and documented purchase facts. Unknown values stay blank. Empty inventory is expected until the owner imports units. |
| 1:45–2:15 | Settings and Anakin health | Session expiry, real configuration, spacing preferences and actual provider activity. Configured credentials do not prove a successful call. |
| 2:15–2:45 | Explain the investigation and case workflow | Anakin finds and extracts approved evidence; deterministic rules evaluate the unit. Missing/conflicting evidence requires review. Holds and tasks support internal follow-up. |

Without genuine inventory, stop at this walkthrough. Do not copy a recalled serial number into an invented owned unit to manufacture a result. The prepared captioned video shows the real deployed UI and empty inventory; it does not claim a live investigation was run during recording.

## With real inventory available

1. Review and import your genuine CSV, or add a unit manually. Use the original purchaser's retailer/country/date when those facts are known; reseller acquisition dates are different facts.
2. In Investigation, select the actual product group and run **Run live Anakin investigation**. It consumes real provider requests and may take up to the current 240-second provider budget. For a short presentation, investigate in advance and identify the timestamp of the saved result, or show the real wait.
3. Inspect the source URLs, exact excerpts, criteria and per-unit outcome. An affected result creates an internal hold and tasks. Incomplete facts can correctly produce review instead. No outcome is promised in advance.
4. Open the unit case, show the explanation and export its action packet or hold list. Acknowledgment never releases a hold automatically. Wire enrichment belongs to the selected case and cannot infer the physical unit's serial.
5. Explain that monitors begin paused. Scheduled monitoring and external signed delivery remain unverified.

Judges can browse the public catalogue without your owner key. Demonstrate private functions through your recording or screen share. Do not include the owner-access file in submission materials.
